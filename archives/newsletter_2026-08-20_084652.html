1. Today's Take

If you were watching the boards yesterday, you saw exactly $6.62M shift hands across 26 six-figure cars.

Bring a Trailer absolutely owned the day, locking down 25 of those completed sales. Cars & Bids grabbed a single piece of the pie with a highly optioned 992-generation GT3.

The big takeaway here is that serious buyers are still heavily liquid and ready to pay up for the right specifications. When a clean, well-documented modern classic drops online, the market responds instantly with zero hesitation.

2. Vehicle Spotlight of the Day

2005 Porsche Carrera GT

  • Sold Price: $3,721,200
  • Auction House: duPont REGISTRY Live
  • Key Takeaway: The ultimate analog supercar.

This particular Carrera GT crossed the block during the duPont REGISTRY Live special Car Week drops, securing a massive $3.72M result. It is a striking reminder of where the smart money goes when macro conditions get noisy.

With its naturally aspirated V10, wooden shift knob, and complete lack of electronic driver aids, the CGT remains the absolute holy grail for collectors seeking a pure mechanical connection. Clean, low-mileage examples like this are comfortably holding their ground in the mid-$3M range, showing no signs of slowing down.

4. What to Watch

Front-Engine V12 Ferraris are Finding Their Forever Homes

The 2019 Ferrari 812 Superfast that sold yesterday on Bring a Trailer for $573,500 proves that collectors are actively preserving the last of the naturally aspirated front-engine V12s. With hybrid powertrains and smaller engines dominating the new-car landscape, the 812 Superfast is transitioning from a depreciating supercar into a highly coveted modern classic. If you want one, buy it now; the window for sub-$400k clean examples is firmly shut.

High-End Replicas Command Serious Capital

Yesterday's sale of a Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR Replica 6-Speed for $417,500 is a perfect lesson in execution. While replica is often a dirty word in some purist circles, a bespoke, highly engineered homage with a modern manual gearbox and flawless metalwork can easily pull genuine supercar money. Buyers are valuing the ability to actually drive and enjoy the legendary design without the stress of exposing a nine-figure museum piece to public roads.

5. High-End Price Breakdown & Valuation Spread (7-Day Rolling Context)

The bulk of the high-end market's liquidity is heavily concentrated in the $100k to $250k tier, accounting for 94 of the 141 total transactions this week. As you move north of $500k, the market thins out quickly, leaving only the most pristine, historically significant models to fight for seven-figure status.

7. What's Coming Up This Week

We are right in the thick of the Monterey Car Week calendar, and the entire collector car community has its eyes fixed on the peninsula. Between the mega-million-dollar live catalog auctions from the likes of RM Sotheby's, Gooding & Company, and Broad Arrow, the physical market is ready to put up some historic numbers.

But do not let the coastal noise distract you from the digital space. Historically, Car Week is a fantastic time to hunt for online deals. With major collectors, dealers, and high-net-worth bidders physically walking the grass in Pebble Beach, online platforms can occasionally experience a dip in active attention, leading to softer reserves and missed bids. If you have cash on the sidelines, stay glued to your alerts over the next few days.
