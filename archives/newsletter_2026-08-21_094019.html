1. Today's Take

Yesterday was proof that post-Monterey liquidity is finding its way straight back into the online markets. While the physical tents on the California peninsula have packed up, the serious money is still out here hunting, dropping $5,677,213 across 21 six-figure sales yesterday alone. It is clear that the buyers who sat on their hands during the live auction bidding wars last week are now hitting the keyboards, ready to lock in clean assets.

The daily volume velocity trend lines reveal a highly consistent rhythm. Despite the expected mid-week dip, yesterday's action held firm at a healthy $5.68 million. We are seeing strong baseline demand for well-sorted analog hardware and high-spec modern track specials, proving that quality continues to dictate pricing even as the summer heat begins to cool.

2021 McLaren 765LT

  • Sold Price: $635,250 USD
  • Auction House: duPont REGISTRY Live

Outfitted with high-dollar MSO options, this car represents a phenomenal buy-in point for a machine that can comfortably embarrass multi-million dollar hypercars on any racetrack. The market for 765LTs has stabilized from its initial post-launch speculation, making highly optioned, clean-history cars like this a very smart place for collectors to put capital.

4. What to Watch (Auctions Closing Today & Tomorrow)

With the physical auctions in the rearview mirror, focus shifts back to active online lots. Keep a close eye on modern GT products and manual-converted supercars closing today and tomorrow. There is a clear pattern emerging where buyers are actively targeting cars with high options lists or documented mechanical conversions.

Specifically, watch how bidding behaves on clean, late-model air-cooled Porsches and modern manual GT3s. If the bids start stalling below reserve, it tells us the post-Monterey buyer pool is being highly selective. But if we see rapid-fire bidding in the final minutes on three-pedal setups, it is proof that the manual premium is still expanding.

5. High-End Price Breakdown & Valuation Spread (7-Day Rolling Context)

The high-end volume over the past week shows a highly top-heavy distribution, though the engine room of the market remains firmly in the $100k to $250k range. That single bracket represents 94 out of 138 total six-figure sales. Once you push past the half-million-dollar mark, transactions get thinner, but the presence of seven-figure entries like the Carrera GT shows that buyers are still willing to step up for blue-chip provenance.

7. What's Coming Up This Week

The dust has settled on the physical Monterey lawns, and we are entering the traditional late-August digest phase. Historically, this is when smart collectors who missed out on the live auctions redirect their capital back to digital catalogs. Bring a Trailer, duPont REGISTRY Live, and Cars & Bids are loading up their boards with fresh inventory to capture this post-event momentum.

For the next 7 days, my advice is simple: look for high-spec modern cars that might have been overshadowed by the multi-million dollar hypercar hype last week. Buyers are still active, but the frenzy of the peninsula is gone, meaning you can bid with a cooler head. Focus on cars with deep option sheets and bulletproof histories.
