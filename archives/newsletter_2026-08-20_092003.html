1. Today's Take

Yesterday saw $6.63M shift hands across 26 six-figure lots online. Bring a Trailer dominated the action, swallowing 96% of the daily volume ($6.36M), while Cars & Bids grabbed the remaining slice with a clean 2023 Porsche 911 GT3.

The post-Monterey dust is officially settling. While the physical tents in Pebble Beach have packed up and the transport trucks are heading home, the digital boards are absorbing the spillover energy. Buyers who didn't raise their paddles in California are putting their capital to work online, especially on modern supercars and analog landmarks.

Yesterday's mid-week velocity shows that the high-end collector market remains liquid and highly reactive, with buyers willing to pay up for the right specifications without hesitation.

2. Vehicle Spotlight of the Day

Yesterday's top sale, the 2019 Porsche 911 GT2 RS Weissach ($907,500), was a massive result, but since its listing did not feature public photo assets, we are shifting the spotlight to the ultimate pinnacle of the past seven days.

2005 Porsche Carrera GT

  • Sold Price: $3,721,200
  • Auction House: duPont REGISTRY Live
  • Sale Date: August 15, 2026

This spectacular $3.72M result for the legendary Carrera GT closed during the duPont REGISTRY Live Monterey Car Week Edition flash sales. As a naturally aspirated V10 manual-gearbox masterpiece, this carbon-fiber icon remains the ultimate benchmark for modern analog supercars. This sale reinforces that even in the high-seven-figure space, the appetite for pure, unmodified driver's cars is stronger than ever.

4. What to Watch (Auctions Closing Today & Tomorrow)

With the live physical auctions in the rearview mirror, collector focus returns to the digital boards where several high-interest lots are closing over the next 48 hours.

Modern Rennsport Performance: Post-Monterey Benchmarks

Watch the late-model Porsche GT cars closing today. With physical auction rooms in California showing mixed results for common specs but massive numbers for rare ones, look at how the digital market prices paint-to-sample (PTS) 991 and 992-generation GT3s. Bidders are looking for buying opportunities if online sellers are realistic about their reserves after seeing physical auction data.

The Rise of High-Tier Replica and Conversion Builds

Following the massive $417,500 result for yesterday's Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR Replica and the $708,500 sale of a gated-converted Murcielago LP640 Roadster on August 18, the market is placing an enormous premium on bespoke engineering. Watch any converted manual exotic or high-end replica closing today. Bidders are treating these highly usable, high-grade builds as legitimate alternatives to investment-grade collector cars.

7. What's Coming Up This Week

Now that we have passed the mid-August peak of live catalog sales, we enter a brief period of market digestion. Sellers who chose not to pay the massive live-auction entry and transport fees for Pebble Beach are actively capitalizing on the heightened buyer attention right now, dropping fresh inventory on digital platforms.

This weekend will be an excellent opportunity for smart buyers to hunt for sleepers. While many collectors are suffering from "Monterey fatigue" and stepping away from their screens, several high-quality, six-figure online auctions will close over the next few days. Keep your eyes on the boards, keep your bids rational, and be ready to move when a clean car slips under the radar.
