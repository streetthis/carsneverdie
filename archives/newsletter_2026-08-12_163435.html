🎙️ 1. Today's Take

The high-end collector market is showing serious strength, with $44.19 million in total volume across 168 six-figure sales in the past seven days. While the bulk of the action remains in the highly liquid $100k to $250k bracket, the top tier is proving that collectors with real capital are highly active. Buyers are being selective, but when a low-mileage blue-chip car or a vehicle with documented provenance hits the block, the bidding is fierce and competitive.

Looking at our daily volume trajectory below, we saw a massive spike on Thursday, August 6, with daily volume hitting $11.27 million. This single-day surge was anchored by a major seven-figure hypercar transaction closing on duPont REGISTRY Live, alongside a steady stream of six-figure Porsche and Ferrari results on BaT. The capital is there; it is simply waiting for the right cars to chase.

👁️ 3. What to Watch

Restomod Kingpins and Bespoke Premiums

The sale of the 1989 Porsche 911 Carrera Classic Turbo by Singer at $1,668,500 proves that early, high-quality bespoke builds are completely decoupled from normal classic car valuations. Collectors aren't looking at these as modified 911s; they're viewing them as modern art. If you are holding an early Singer commission, the market is highly receptive to seven-figure exits right now.

Race Pedigree with a License Plate

The road-legal 1988 Porsche Kremer 962 Group C Endurance Race Car pulling over $1.08 million highlights a growing trend: serious collectors want historical race cars they can actually drive on the street. Pure track cars can be tough to sell because track access is a chore, but road-registered legends with Le Mans and Andretti history are highly sought after and command massive premiums.

📈 4. High-End Price Breakdown & Valuation Spread

Six-figure market volume is heavily weighted toward the entry-level tier. Out of the 168 cars sold above $100,000, a massive 122 of them fell in the $100k–$250k range, where Bring a Trailer captured 101 of those sales.

However, as we move into the ultra-high-end tiers, the market share shifts. While Bring a Trailer still handles the majority of the $250k–$500k bracket (28 of 30 units), boutique platforms like duPont REGISTRY Live are effectively capturing the top of the pyramid, owning the single $2M+ sale of the week and taking a healthy slice of the $500k–$1M segments.
