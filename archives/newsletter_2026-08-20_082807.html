DAILY COLLECTOR CAR MARKET INTELLIGENCE • BY DREW

1. Today's Take

Yesterday’s action across high-end online auctions generated a healthy 26 transactions over the six-figure threshold, representing a combined daily volume of $6,628,926.35. Bring a Trailer absorbed nearly the entire daily share with 25 sales, while Cars & Bids rounded out the action with a single high-profile GT3. It was a highly active Tuesday that proved that despite late summer distractions, serious buyer capital is actively changing hands for prime machinery.

The clear story of the day was the strong performance of modern track-focused weapons and low-mileage V12 flagships. We are seeing a steady floor for top-tier modern performance, with buyers stepping up to hand over significant premiums for highly optioned cars. Let's look at the numbers and chart the trajectory of this high-end activity.

2. Vehicle Spotlight of the Day

Vehicle Spotlight of the Day

2019 Porsche 911 GT2 RS Weissach
* Hammer Price: $907,500
* Auction House: Bring a Trailer
* Spec & Mileage: Finished in GT Silver Metallic over black and red leather, carrying only 1,100 miles, and equipped with the highly desirable Weissach Package.

Yesterday's top sale was a textbook example of why the GT2 RS remains the king of the water-cooled Rennsport line. At $907,500, this result demonstrates that collectors still view this 700-horsepower rear-wheel-drive monster as a true blue-chip modern asset. With the Weissach Package lopping off weight via carbon fiber and magnesium components, this spec represents the absolute peak of the 991 generation's track capability.

While some late-model supercars have faced mild corrections over the last year, the GT2 RS continues to hold its value with exceptional strength. Buyers are willing to pay massive premiums for delivery-mileage, highly optioned examples of Stuttgart's ultimate track weapon.

4. What to Watch

Modern Front-Engine V12 Premium

The 2019 Ferrari 812 Superfast fetched an impressive $573,500 yesterday. This is an incredibly strong number for an 812, showing that collectors are increasingly eager to lock in clean, front-engine naturally aspirated V12s. With Ferrari shifting its lineup toward hybrid and turbocharged powertrains, clean, low-mileage 812s are decoupling from standard depreciation curves and moving firmly into modern classic territory.

The High-End Recreation Play

A stunning Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR Replica 6-Speed commanded a staggering $417,500. This result demonstrates that buyers will spend serious money for flawless craftsmanship and usability. For collectors who love the lines of a classic silver arrow but want a modern, reliable powertrain and manual gearbox, these top-tier custom builds represent a highly attractive alternative to high-maintenance originals.

5. High-End Price Breakdown & Valuation Spread (7-Day Rolling Context)

Over the past seven days, the sub-$250k tier remains the high-volume foundation of the collector market, accounting for 94 of the 141 total six-figure sales. However, we are seeing a steady flow of transactions moving through the mid-to-high tiers, with 29 sales in the $250k-$500k range and another 15 sales crossing the half-million-dollar mark. This spread indicates a healthy, multi-tiered marketplace where buyers feel confident across all valuation levels.

7. What's Coming Up This Week

As Monterey Car Week previews loom and flagship offline events approach, online bidding is keeping pace. Daily drops on duPont REGISTRY Live and Bring a Trailer will continue to test the limits of modern hypercars. Serious collectors should watch the action closely to gauge broader market sentiment before the major catalog auctions begin.

Keep an eye out for any unusual pricing spikes or unexpected passes on high-reserve lots over the next few days. With so many high-end vehicles hitting the market simultaneously, we will likely see some highly strategic bidding maneuvers as collectors look to deploy their capital.
