WHY I DID NOT SLEEP LAST NIGHT

Yesterday was a masterclass in how post-Monterey capital finds its home online once the physical tents fold up. We watched $5.81M trade hands across 22 six-figure cars yesterday, and the bidding didn't feel like a late-summer wind-down—it felt like a clean redirection.

Buyers who walked away empty-handed from the Monterey peninsula are clearly sitting on live cash, and they are not waiting until the fall to deploy it. Porsche and Lamborghini led the charge, proving that high-spec modern performance and analog legends are where the smart money is landing.
