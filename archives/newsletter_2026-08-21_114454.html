WHY I DID NOT SLEEP LAST NIGHT

Yesterday was a loud reminder that the post-Monterey capital has not dried up. We saw $5,813,713.05 shift hands across 22 six-figure cars yesterday, pushing our 7-day rolling total to $38,691,571.85 across 139 completed transactions.

The live-auction dust in California has settled, but the liquidity didn't leave the building. High-end collectors who went home empty-handed from the peninsula are taking their remaining capital straight to the digital boards. The online platforms are absorbing that leftover demand without missing a beat.

Yesterday’s trading was a mix of vintage analog dominance and high-ticket modern track weapons. Bring a Trailer cleared an elite 1973 Porsche 911 Carrera RS Lightweight at $812,500 and a gated-spec 2007 Lamborghini Murcielago LP640 Coupe for $707,500, while duPont REGISTRY Live secured our spotlight car of the day with a stunning Curacao Blue 2021 McLaren 765LT at $635,250. The appetite for premium, highly optioned machinery remains incredibly healthy.

WHAT TO WATCH

With Monterey Car Week officially wrapped up, all eyes are back on the digital boards today and tomorrow to see if this momentum holds through the weekend.

Post-Monterey Manual Momentum

Following yesterday's $707,500 result for the 2007 Lamborghini Murcielago LP640 Coupe on Bring a Trailer, keep a close eye on any manual-conversion or original gated V10 and V12 supercars closing this weekend. The market is aggressively rewarding three-pedal setups, and buyers who missed out on live-auction lots in California are actively hunt-mode for manual conversion projects.

Rennsport Volume Check

We saw a massive influx of modern Porsches cross the block yesterday, including a 2026 GT3 Touring Lightweight 6-Speed at $320,740. Watch the high-end water-cooled Porsches closing today and tomorrow to see if the market can continue to absorb this volume, or if we will start to see buyers getting selective on specs and colors as supply remains highly active.

WHAT'S COMING UP THIS WEEK

This late-August weekend is historically one of the busiest times for digital platforms. High-end dealers are actively clearing space on their showroom floors after a busy Monterey week, and private collectors are eager to deploy their liquid capital. Expect a massive catalog drop on both Bring a Trailer and duPont REGISTRY Live over the next 48 hours.

For buyers looking to strike a deal, keep a close watch on high-reserve lots closing this Sunday. Many sellers who missed the spotlight in California are highly motivated to close deals online, meaning there will be excellent opportunities to step in and negotiate post-auction if the bidding falls just short of the reserve.
