*2. Vehicle Spotlight of the Day*

While the GT2 RS grabbed the top spot by pure dollar value yesterday, the real eyebrow-raiser was this clean 2019 Ferrari 812 Superfast. Standard, non-limited 812s typically trade in the high-$300k to low-$400k range. This particular example blew past those expectations, hammering for an incredible $573,500.

* *Vehicle*: 2019 Ferrari 812 Superfast (https://bringatrailer.com/listing/2019-ferrari-812-superfast-31/)
* *Hammer Price*: $573,500 USD
* *Auction House*: Bring a Trailer
* *The Takeaway*: This result shows that collectors are placing an increasingly high premium on original-owner, low-mileage V12 front-engine grand tourers. With Ferrari pushing harder into hybrid assistance across its newer lineups, the pure, naturally aspirated 6.5-liter V12 in the 812 Superfast is rapidly graduating into a modern classic. Buyers are paying up for high-option builds, original paint, and flawless documentation.

*4. What to Watch (Auctions Closing Today & Tomorrow)*

With Monterey wrapped up, the focus returns to the digital boards where a few key trends are playing out over the next 48 hours:

*Gated Manuals and Analog V12s*
Following yesterday's $247,500 sale of the 2000 Ferrari 550 Maranello (https://bringatrailer.com/listing/2000-ferrari-550-maranello-67/), watch active analog gated 6-speed V12 listings closing today and tomorrow. There is a clear flight to safety in analog cars with three pedals. Keep a close eye on bidding momentum in the final hours to see if buyers are willing to push past standard reserves for well-sorted driver-mileage examples.

*997-Generation Porsche GT3 Momentum*
Yesterday's 2007 Porsche 911 GT3 (https://bringatrailer.com/listing/2007-porsche-911-gt3-109/) hammered for $260,750, showcasing the strong premium remaining for clean 997-era Rennsport and GT models. If you are watching active listings today or tomorrow, pay close attention to over-rev reports and paint meter readings. Clean-sheet cars are commanding big premiums, while heavily modified or tracked examples are struggling to meet reserve.

*7. What's Coming Up This Week*

Now that the dust has settled on the physical Monterey auctions, the next seven days are all about digital consolidation.
