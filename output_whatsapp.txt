*WHY I DID NOT SLEEP LAST NIGHT*

Yesterday was a loud reminder that while the physical tents in Monterey have packed up and left, the high-end online market isn't taking a single breath. We watched $5.67M trade hands across 21 six-figure sales yesterday, and the action was incredibly telling.

If you thought a post-Monterey hangover would quiet down the bidding, think again. From an $812k air-cooled RS lightweight to a $707k gated-era V12 Lambo, the smart money is still moving fast, aggressively targeting limited-run modern classics and analogue beasts that are getting harder to find in any market condition.

##### Vehicle Spotlight
*2021 McLaren 765LT*

While standard 720S prices continue to face typical modern supercar depreciation, the limited-run 765LT is proving to be a genuine store of value. Bidders recognized the rarity of this spec and paid up for a highly documented example. At $635,250, it represents a strong, stable marker for McLaren's ultimate series models in the current secondary market.
