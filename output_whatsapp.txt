*WHY I DID NOT SLEEP LAST NIGHT*

Yesterday was a loud reminder that while the physical tents in Monterey have packed up and left, the high-end online market isn't taking a single breath. We watched $5.67M trade hands across 21 six-figure sales yesterday, and the action was incredibly telling. 

If you thought a post-Monterey hangover would quiet down the bidding, think again. From an $812k air-cooled RS lightweight to a $707k gated-era V12 Lambo, the smart money is still moving fast, aggressively targeting limited-run modern classics and analogue beasts that are getting harder to find in any market condition.

*VEHICLE SPOTLIGHT*

*2021 McLaren 765LT (https://live.dupontregistry.com/auction/2021-mclaren-765ltcuracaobluemsooptions-3718)*
* *Hammer Price:* $635,250 USD
* *Platform:* duPont REGISTRY Live
* *Color / Spec:* MSO Curacao Blue over Carbon Black
* *Milestone Specs:* Twin-turbo 4.0L V8, 755 hp, 1-of-765 globally

[Chart: 2021 McLaren 765LT]

The 765LT represents everything McLaren does best when they stop worrying about grand touring comfort and focus strictly on track violence. It is raw, incredibly light (shedding 176 lbs over the already insane 720S), and has gear ratios short enough to make your head spin. 

While the standard 720S has taken the usual heavy McLaren depreciation hit, the limited-run 765LT is a completely different story. Commanded by collectors for its scarcity, this MSO Curacao Blue example hammered for a massive $635,250. This sale proves that buyers aren't afraid of modern supercars when they carry that exclusive LT badge and a heavy MSO build sheet.

*WHAT TO WATCH*

We are looking at active auctions closing today and tomorrow as we head into the weekend. Keep a close eye on two massive pockets of momentum:

*The Analogue V12 Escalation*
Following yesterday's massive $707k result for the 2007 Lamborghini Murcielago LP640 Coupe (https://bringatrailer.com/listing/2007-lamborghini-murcielago-lp640-coupe-11/), watch any late-2000s analogue supercars closing today. *Modern GT Performance in the Post-Monterey Void*
With some buyers going home empty-handed from California, watch for aggressive bidding on highly optioned late-model Porsche GT cars closing today and tomorrow. Collectors who missed out are highly motivated to deploy capital on clean, delivery-mileage specs online.
