*WHY I DID NOT SLEEP LAST NIGHT*

If you were watching the boards yesterday, you saw $5.68M shift hands across 21 six-figure cars. The physical dust from Monterey is finally settling, but the digital space is absorbing the leftover heat. Buyers who went to California with open checkbooks and left empty-handed are clearly not waiting around for the autumn live auctions; they are deploying capital online right now.

Yesterday was dominated by air-cooled icons and gated-shifting V12s. We watched a 1973 Porsche 911 Carrera RS Lightweight pull a massive $812,500 on BaT, while a pristine 2007 Lamborghini Murcielago LP640 Coupe commanded $707,500. This is not panic buying. It is smart money targeting highly optioned, low-mileage modern classics that are increasingly difficult to source in the wild.

*WHAT TO WATCH*

With the physical auctions in California behind us, the digital marketplace is the best place to track real-time collector sentiment over the next 48 hours. Here are two areas to watch closing today and tomorrow:

1. *Air-Cooled Porsche Momentum*: Following the solid performance of yesterday's 1997 Porsche 911 Turbo at $385,500 and the Carrera RS at $812,500, pay close attention to several late-model 993 and 964 Turbos closing today on Bring a Trailer. If these clear their reserves with strong bidding, it proves that the vintage Porsche market has shrugged off any summer stagnation.
2. *Modern Supercar Price Floors*: Watch the low-mileage Ferrari F8 and McLaren 720S lots closing on Cars & Bids and Bring a Trailer. These mid-engine modern platforms have experienced steady downward pressure over the past year, but we are beginning to see solid floors established. Look for active bidding on clean, well-documented examples to confirm if the slide is finally flattening out.

*WHAT'S COMING UP THIS WEEK*

With Monterey Car Week officially in the rearview mirror, we are entering the classic late-August transition. The live, physical catalogs are pausing briefly before the fall auctions, which means the spotlight shifts entirely to the digital platforms. Over the coming weekend, we expect major late-summer catalog drops on Bring a Trailer, duPont REGISTRY Live, and Cars & Bids, where sellers who held back during the Monterey madness are now listing their inventory to capture unspent buyer capital.

If you are a serious collector, use this weekend to scour these fresh listings. Many underbidders from the physical auctions in California are actively scanning the online boards, creating a highly competitive but liquid environment. Look for premium modern classics and limited-run GTs to lead the charge as we close out the summer.
